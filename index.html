We build and run the technology your business depends on.

Most of our work starts with a real, slightly messy problem: a system nobody wants to touch, a migration that keeps slipping, a spreadsheet that quietly runs the company. We help you sort it out and then stick around to make sure it holds.

What a first conversation looks like

  1. A short call, no slideshow. You tell us what is going on and we ask a lot of questions.
  2. A written summary. A few days later you get a plain note on what we heard and what we would tackle first.
  3. An honest answer. If we are the right people we will scope the work. If we are not, we will say so.

Questions

Things people usually ask first.

How do we get started?

Send us a message below or email us. We will set up a short call, listen, and follow up with a written summary of next steps. There is no charge for that first conversation.

Can you work with our existing team and tools?

Yes, and we prefer to. We fit in around the people and systems you already have, and we document everything so your team can take over whenever they want.

How do you handle confidential information?

We work under signed confidentiality terms from day one, give people only the access they need and keep client data out of personal accounts and devices.

Do you only take large projects?

No. Plenty of our work starts small, such as a review, a pilot or a single integration. We are happy to start there and see whether it makes sense to continue.

What happens after the project is finished?

You own the work and the documentation. If you want us to stay involved, we offer support arrangements. If you would rather run it yourselves, we will make that handover as smooth as we can.
